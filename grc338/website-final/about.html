Welcome to Lucky Slice Pizza, where family and great food come together in the heart of Pismo Beach! We are bursting with pride as a third-generation, family-owned and operated restaurant that has captured the taste buds and hearts of our beloved community. Our journey began in 1987 when our Pappou and Yiayia, known to all as Kathy and Jimmy, opened the doors of ‘Lucky Pizza’ on Pier Avenue. Little did they know, they were about to create a legacy.

As time went on, our parents, along with our aunt and uncle, assumed the reins of the family business and relocated to our current home on Price Street. With each passing year, Lucky Slice Pizza has woven strong ties with our extended family—the incredible people of the Central Coast.

The dream team of Anna, Matt, Claire, and Maria poured their heart and soul into making Lucky Slice Pizza the go-to spot for our community. We owe a huge slice of gratitude to our unstoppable parents who tirelessly kneaded dough, flipped pizzas, and worked to ensure our bright future.
